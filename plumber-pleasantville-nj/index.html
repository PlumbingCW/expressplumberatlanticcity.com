Slow drains

When one fixture is slow, the clog usually sits in that fixture's own branch. When the tub, the toilet and the kitchen sink all complain at once, the blockage is further down. Drain cleaning starts with a cable or camera so we know which case you have, and we tell you what we see before choosing the next step. If the main line is the culprit, sewer line cleaning is the next conversation.

No hot water

A heater that stops heating is not always a dead tank. A failed element, thermostat or igniter can be repaired, while a tank that leaks from the shell cannot. Water heater repair covers the first group. If the tank itself has failed, we say so plainly and price a replacement as its own job.

Leaks you cannot see

A silent leak shows up as a bill that climbs or a meter that moves when nothing is running. Close every tap, read the meter, and check again after ten or fifteen minutes. If the dial moved, leak detection can trace the line without opening walls at random.

Before We Arrive: five things to do in your Pleasantville home

Find the main shutoff

It is usually where the water line enters the house, near the meter. Turn it clockwise if water is running where it should not.

Isolate the heater

For a leaking tank, close the cold supply valve above it and switch off the breaker on an electric unit. Do not step into standing water to reach anything.

Clear under the sinks

Move boxes from under sinks, around the water heater and away from any drain cleanout so the crew can reach the fixture.

Main Street buildings

In a building with several units, find the shared shutoff and reach the owner or manager, who usually has to approve repairs.

Stay reachable

Keep your phone on and have your house number and cross street ready in case we call back to confirm the address.

Gas smell, sparks or water near outlets: who to call before us

A gas smell sends you outside first, with no light switches and no phone calls from inside. From the sidewalk, call South Jersey Gas at 1-800-582-7060 or 911. We do not promise a response time on a gas problem, and once the utility clears the building we can handle the pipe repair.

Water near an outlet, a panel or a plugged-in appliance is an electrical hazard too. Stay out of the room, shut off the main water if you can reach it dry, and call 911 if you see sparks or smell burning. We handle the plumbing once the area is safe.
