Chelsea, Atlantic City

Plumber in Chelsea, Atlantic City, NJ

Chelsea is one of the named residential neighborhoods on the city's own list, with its own neighborhood association. If a drain, leak, water heater or fixture in a Chelsea home needs a plumber, call (609) 547-1417 and describe the problem. This page explains the area and the questions worth asking before you book.

Where Chelsea sits in Atlantic City

The City of Atlantic City's Living in Atlantic City page lists Chelsea among eight neighborhoods that have a civic or neighborhood association. A University of Pennsylvania planning report describes it as running from Texas Avenue on the north to Albany Avenue on the south, and from the Atlantic Ocean to the Intracoastal Waterway. Residents may draw the edges a little differently, so treat those streets as a guide, not a survey line.

When you call, give your street and the nearest cross street. That settles whether you are in Chelsea, in Chelsea Heights, which is a separate neighborhood with its own association, or in a neighboring area such as Ducktown just to the north.

What the housing looks like

The same report says Chelsea was built out in the late 19th and early 20th centuries, after marshland along the waterway was filled in. It lists rowhouses and twins, small-lot detached houses, basement apartments in multifamily buildings and multifamily flats. It also calls the area one of the places in Atlantic City with the highest historic integrity.

That mix matters for plumbing. Attached houses and flats may share walls, floors or a building drain, so a leak or a backup can involve a neighbor or a landlord. Tell the person on the phone whether you own the building, rent a unit or manage the property. Building age alone does not tell you what pipe is inside it. Only a look at the actual pipes does.

Rain, high tides and filled-in marsh

Because the planning report says Chelsea was built on filled marshland, it helps to know how flooding works in Atlantic City generally. The National Weather Service tide gauge treats 6.0 feet as minor tidal flooding and 7.0 feet as moderate flooding with widespread roadway flooding. When high water backs up through street drains, a floor drain or a low toilet can be the first place it shows.

This is a general point about the city, not a claim about any one street in Chelsea. If drains gurgle or back up during a high tide or storm, stop using water and keep away from electrical items. Ask the city whether the trouble is in the street before you pay to clear a line that is not yours.

Local note

Gas, water and sewer: who to call first

If you smell gas, leave the building on foot and call South Jersey Gas at 1-800-582-7060 or 911 before you call a plumber. Do not use switches or phones inside.

For a water main or street-side water problem, the utility for Atlantic City is the Atlantic City Municipal Utilities Authority, (609) 345-3315. Which agency owns the sewer main on a given block should be confirmed with the city or ACMUA, because it is not the same everywhere.

New homes next to old rowhouses

Jersey Digs reported in September 2026 that the Atlantic City planning board approved plans for nine new single-family homes on North California Avenue in Chelsea. The same report mentions a separate townhouse project nearby. New houses and old rowhouses raise different questions. A new house starts with the builder's warranty and the hookups. An older home starts with shutoff valves and fixtures that may never have been replaced.

Before you call about a Chelsea plumbing problem

  1. 1

    Stop the water

    Close the shutoff valve under the fixture. If water is still running, close the main shutoff and leave it closed until it is sorted out.

  2. 2

    Note who owns what

    Say whether you own the building, rent a unit or manage it. In attached buildings that decides who needs to hear about the problem.

  3. 3

    Write down the symptoms

    A short note on when it started and which fixtures are affected saves time on the phone. A photo of a leak or a stain helps more than a description.

  4. 4

    Have your cross street ready

    Atlantic City addresses can be hard to place from a number alone, so a cross street keeps the conversation short.
