Do these three things while you wait

If the tank is leaking, shut the cold water supply valve on the pipe above the heater by turning the handle a quarter turn or clockwise until it stops. Then cut power at the breaker for an electric unit, or turn the gas control knob to off for a gas unit. Keep towels away from the base and stay clear of any standing water near outlets.

If you smell gas, skip all of that. Leave the building on foot, then call South Jersey Gas at 1-800-582-7060 or 911 before you call a plumber. Do not flip switches or use a phone inside. We never fix a suspected gas leak before the gas utility has made the space safe.

When a leaking water heater is an emergency

A steady drip from the tank body, water pooling in a closet, or a puddle that grows over an hour all count as emergencies. A full tank holds dozens of gallons, and a failed seam can empty it onto the floor, the drywall below, or the unit under you in a condo or apartment building.

Not every wet floor means a dead tank. Water often comes from a loose fitting, a dripping relief valve, or condensation on cold pipes. The table below shows what we check first, because the source decides whether this is a repair or a replacement. Our leak detection work covers cases where the water is not clearly from the heater.

No hot water, weak heat or strange noises

A heater that stops making hot water usually has one failed part. On a gas unit that is often the thermocouple, the pilot assembly or the gas control valve. On an electric unit it is usually a heating element or a thermostat. Hybrid heat pump models add a compressor, fan and sensor, so they get their own diagnosis.

  • Gas tank: pilot will not stay lit, or the burner starts then shuts off.
  • Electric tank: breaker is on but water never gets above lukewarm, or you get hot water for only a few minutes.
  • Popping or rumbling: sediment layered at the bottom of the tank, which a flush may help.
  • Rust-colored hot water only: the tank lining or anode rod may be wearing out. Check cold water first to rule out the pipes.
  • Hybrid model: error light or fan noise with weak heat.

For rust-colored water, run the cold tap for a minute. If cold runs clear and hot is brown, the heater is the likely source. Atlantic City Municipal Utilities Authority reports its water quality each year, and the best way to learn what is in your own supply is to test your tap. We do not guess at water chemistry.

Gas, electric and hybrid units need different repairs

A gas heater repair always includes a combustion and venting check, since a weak draft pushes exhaust into the room. We look for scorching at the draft hood, loose vent joints, and a clear burner. An electric repair starts with a meter on the elements and thermostats, plus a look at the wiring for heat damage.

Many garages, closets and utility rooms in condos and apartments hold a heater squeezed in tight, which makes access the biggest practical issue. If the part cannot be reached safely we say so and explain the options. Planning an upgrade instead of a patch? Our water heater installation page covers choosing a new unit.

Repair or replace: how we help you decide

Look for the date code on the data plate, the silver label on the tank. The first letter and numbers usually encode the month and year of manufacture, and the manufacturer can tell you how to read it. Age alone does not settle the question, so we weigh it against what is failing.

Repair versus replacement at a glance
SituationRepair makes senseReplacement makes sense
Failed part on a tank that is otherwise dryYes, a new element, thermostat or thermocoupleOnly if the tank is very old
Water leaking from the tank itselfNo, a tank leak cannot be patchedYes, see water heater replacement
Repeated repairs on the same unitWeigh the repair against the next failureOften yes
Needs more hot water than the tank givesRepair will not add capacityConsider a larger tank or a tankless unit

We tell you which side of the table your heater is on, and we do not push replacement when a part fixes the problem. A replacement is quoted separately so you can compare.

What affects the cost of water heater repair

Which part failed

A thermostat, element, valve or gas control all carry different part and labor costs.

Access

A heater in an open utility room is simpler than one boxed into a closet or attic.

Fuel and unit type

Gas, electric and hybrid heaters need different parts and different test equipment.

After-hours work

The hour of the call can affect the figure, so confirm it before work starts.

Related damage

Corroded fittings, a bad shutoff valve or a failed drain pan can add work.
