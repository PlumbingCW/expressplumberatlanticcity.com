Service is booked by phone. We give you a price before work starts, based on what we know at that time. If we find something different once we open a wall or run a camera, we explain the change and you decide whether to go ahead. Cost factors listed on this site are explanations, not quotes.

This website is not an emergency service. For a fire, a medical emergency or electrical contact with water, call 911.
