Who to call for the street side of a problem

Atlantic City's water comes from the Atlantic City Municipal Utilities Authority, reached at (609) 345-3315. Ventnor City runs its own water and sewer department at 609-823-7935. Pleasantville, Egg Harbor Township, Absecon and Galloway Township are in New Jersey American Water's Atlantic system, at 1-800-272-1325.

Those agencies handle the main in the street and the meter. Everything past the meter is ours, so a leaking line to the house is a job for water service line repair and a clogged branch is a job for drain cleaning. If you cannot tell which side a sewer problem is on, call us and we will help you work it out.

Not sure whether your problem is ours or the utility's? Call and ask.
