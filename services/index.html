How to choose between similar services

Some problems have two good answers. A single slow drain points to drain cleaning, while every drain in the building backing up points to sewer line cleaning. Scale or grease that keeps coming back is usually a job for hydro jetting, and a camera run through sewer inspection shows what is inside the pipe before anyone digs.

Water heaters follow the same logic. A failed part or a leaking valve is a repair, while a rusted tank is a replacement. Planned swaps and upgrades belong with water heater installation and tankless water heaters.
