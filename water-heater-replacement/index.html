A failed tank tonight: what to do before the plumber is on the way

Close the cold water valve above the heater and switch the breaker or gas control to off. If the tank is still leaking, open a hot tap on a lower floor to let the pressure drop, and set a bucket or shop vacuum at the base. Move boxes, laundry and anything electrical off the floor.

Take two photos for us, one of the data plate and one of the whole heater with its pipes. They tell us the fuel, the size and the vent type before we load the truck. A gas smell changes everything. Leave on foot, then call South Jersey Gas at 1-800-582-7060 or 911 first.

Is it really time to replace the tank?

Water coming out of the tank body or its seam means the steel has failed, and no repair will hold. Other signs are repeated part failures, rust-colored hot water with a clear cold line, and a heater whose date code puts it past the age the manufacturer expects. If a single part failed, see water heater repair first.

We do not push replacement when a part fixes the problem. If it is a close call, we tell you what the cheaper repair buys and what the next failure might cost. That is your decision, with the numbers in front of you.

Picking the right size and type

Size follows how many people shower at once and how many fixtures run together. A tank that was too small for the household the first time will be too small again, so mention if your family has grown. A bigger tank also needs more floor space and sometimes a larger vent.

Like-for-like or upgrade

Same-type tank

Fastest swap. Same fuel, same location, usually the same connections.

High-efficiency tank

Better insulation or a power vent. May need new venting or an outlet.

Heat pump hybrid

Electric unit that pulls heat from the air. Needs room clearance and a drain, and we discuss it as an option.

Tankless

Smaller wall unit with a bigger gas line and vent. A planned project, covered on the tankless water heater page.

Condos, apartments and tight closets

In a multi-story building the work depends on the building as much as the heater. Ask your property manager about elevator reservations, parking for a work van, and any rule about shutting water to the riser. A heater in an interior closet may need to be carried around a corner, and we flag that in the walk-through.

If the tank has flooded a unit below you, call your property manager at once. They may need to shut off the riser or inspect the ceiling below. We do not assess structural or mold damage. Insurers and restoration firms handle those parts.

What a good replacement quote lists

  • Unit brand, capacity and fuel type, plus the model number.
  • New connectors, shutoff valve, drain pan and relief valve discharge pipe.
  • Expansion tank, if your system needs one.
  • Venting changes for gas units, or electrical changes for electric ones.
  • Haul-away of the old tank.
  • Permit handling, if your local construction office asks for one. Rules vary by job.

If you are weighing a new location or fuel switch, see water heater installation for planned-project details. A clean swap in the same place is the quickest.

What affects the cost of a water heater replacement

Tank size and type

A larger tank, a high-efficiency model or a heat pump hybrid costs more than a standard tank.

Venting or electrical changes

A gas unit with worn venting or an electric one needing a new circuit adds work.

Access and haul-out

Stairs, narrow doors and a long carry increase labor time.

Code-related parts

Expansion tanks, drain pans and relief valve piping depend on your setup.

After-hours work

Ask whether a night or weekend swap changes the figure, and confirm it before work starts.

A tank that just burst may be handled differently from one that simply wore out. Ask your insurer before you file.
