Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Atlantic City Express Plumbing receives when you call us, email us or visit this website, and how we use it.

Call (609) 547-1417All services

Information we receive

When you call (609) 547-1417, we receive your phone number and whatever you tell us, such as your name, service address and a description of the problem. When you email contact@expressplumberatlanticcity.com, we receive your email address and the content of your message.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the person who answers at the start of the call.

How we use it

We use this information to schedule and perform plumbing work, to give you a price, to follow up on a job, to handle billing and to improve how we answer calls. We do not sell your personal information.

This website

This site has no contact forms, no accounts and no advertising or analytics scripts that follow you across the web. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and pages requested, for security and reliability.

Our pages embed Google Maps. When a map loads, Google may collect information under its own privacy policy. We do not control that collection.

Because we do not collect data through the website itself, the information above comes from you directly. It helps us to understand what a service call involves before we get to the property, and to keep a record of work done so we can answer questions about it later.

Sharing

We share information only as needed to do the job and run the business. That can include suppliers who need a model or part number, payment processors, insurers, and authorities when the law requires it.

Keeping and correcting information

We keep job records for as long as we need them for service history, billing and legal reasons. To ask what we hold about you, to correct it or to ask us to delete it where the law allows, email contact@expressplumberatlanticcity.com.

Children

Our services are for property owners, tenants and businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Questions about this policy can go to contact@expressplumberatlanticcity.com or to 1616 Pacific Ave, Atlantic City, NJ 08401. You can also read our terms of use or return to the contact page.

Where we work around Atlantic City

Dispatch runs from 1616 Pacific Ave in Atlantic City, NJ 08401, with service to Pleasantville, Egg Harbor Township, Ventnor City, Absecon and Galloway Township.
