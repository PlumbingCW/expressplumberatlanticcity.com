Before We Arrive: what a Galloway household should have ready

Know your community and route

Pomona, Smithville, Oceanville or another community, plus the nearest main road. Distances in the township are long, so this matters.

Know your water source

Say if you are on a meter from the street or on a private well. If you are unsure, look for a meter box near the curb or a pressure tank in the basement or utility room.

Shut the main

Turn the main shutoff clockwise if water is leaking inside. On a well system, switch off the pump breaker as well.

Clear a path outside

For a yard leak, move vehicles, toys and planters from the wet area, and mark it. Do not dig yourself.

Protect the finished spaces

Lift rugs and boxes off wet floors, and photograph everything before you clean up.

When patching stops making sense: repiping

Repeated pinhole leaks, discolored water and low pressure at several fixtures can point to pipes at the end of their life, and the age of the plumbing in your house is the real question. Repiping replaces the failing runs, and we price the work clearly before it starts. A single leak in an otherwise sound line can often be patched instead.

Water heaters, planned swaps and leaking tanks

A new heater is a planned job when the old one is still working. Water heater installation covers venting, expansion tanks and a code check, and same-day appointments are available when the schedule allows. If the old tank is already leaking, call the 24/7 line. A failing heater is an emergency when water is on the floor.

Pinelands, permits and who to call about what

Part of Galloway lies in the Pinelands National Reserve. Rules for digging and outdoor work can differ there, so ask the township construction office before planning a yard excavation. New Jersey's Uniform Construction Code governs plumbing, and the local construction official enforces it. Permits vary by job, and we do not guess which ones you need.

A gas smell means leaving on foot and calling South Jersey Gas at 1-800-582-7060 or 911 from outside. We do not promise a response time on gas problems. You can check any plumber through the NJ State Board of Examiners of Master Plumbers, and we are licensed and insured. If a well pump or pressure tank failed from wear, ask your insurer whether it is covered, because policies usually cover sudden and accidental damage and not maintenance.
